Why we changed what we’re raising money for

October 23, 2026

When we started Upahar Giving, the plan was simple. The teachers at the ashrama are paid much less than teachers in government schools, and the school struggles to find even that. Our first campaign asked people to help cover their pay.

As we worked out how to do that properly, we spent a lot of time with India’s Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act. Under it, a charity in India can only receive money from abroad if it is registered to, and the ashrama isn’t. Paying the school’s own teachers from the United States to keep doing their jobs could fairly be seen as funding the school by another route. There may be ways to structure that so it holds up, but we didn’t want the school’s future to rest on an arrangement designed to get around the rules.

So we’ve changed course. We still want to support the school and the people who teach there. They are a big part of why the children do as well as they do. What has changed is how. Upahar now runs its own programs at the school, focused on the children’s learning, and wherever we can, we look to work with NGOs already active in Karnataka. Over time we’d like to help the ashrama get its own registration, so it can receive support from abroad directly and on its own terms.

That is slower than writing a cheque, and we know it. It is also the only approach we’re comfortable asking you to fund.

If you’ve already given

Thank you. Gifts support Upahar’s programs, and our board decides how funds are used. If you gave to our first campaign and want to know how your gift will be used, or have any other questions, write to us at upahargiving@gmail.com.
