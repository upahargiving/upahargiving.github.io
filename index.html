A better education for the children at a small school in Karnataka, where most have no home to return to.

Shree Shiva Shaikshanika Seva Ashrama is a residential school near Tumakuru. It houses, feeds and teaches children from 1st to 10th standard. About 90% of them have no parents or no safe home. Upahar Giving runs its own programs at the school to help the children learn well.

The school

Mr. Lepakshaiah worked as a forest guard near Tumakuru. He had no money and no standing to speak of, but he believed everyone owes something to the people around them. He wanted a place where children without a family could get food, a bed and an education.

Someone gave him a small plot of land and a modest building, free. In 1995 he opened a residential school in it with almost nothing else. It is still running.

What we care about

Two things: that the children have what they need to learn well while they are at the school, and that they leave able to read, write and do arithmetic well enough to stand on their own. What they need changes from year to year, so we would rather do a few things well than promise many.

Wherever we can, we look to work with NGOs already active in Karnataka. They know the local schools, the language and the rules far better than a few volunteers in New Jersey do, and the children are better served by people who can be there every week.

How it works

Upahar Giving runs its own programs at the school. The ashrama hosts them and helps us on the ground, but we don’t send money to the school: Indian law restricts foreign funds to registered charities, and the ashrama isn’t registered to receive them.

We take no commission and have no paid staff. Gifts support Upahar’s programs, and our board decides how funds are used. Read the partnership brief for the details.

About us

Upahar Giving Inc. is a volunteer-run nonprofit in New Jersey. Upahar means “gift” in Hindi.
