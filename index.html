Help pay the teachers at a small school in Karnataka where most children have no home to return to.

Shree Shiva Shaikshanika Seva Ashrama is a residential school near Tumakuru. It houses, feeds and teaches children from 1st to 10th standard. About 90% of them have no parents or no safe home. The hardest bill to pay each month is the teachers’ salaries.

The school

Mr. Lepakshaiah worked as a forest guard near Tumakuru. He had no money and no standing to speak of, but he believed everyone owes something to the people around them. He wanted a place where children without a family could get food, a bed and an education.

Someone gave him a small plot of land and a modest building, free. In 1995 he opened a residential school in it with almost nothing else. It is still running.

The problem

Even ₹10,000 a month is hard for the ashrama to find. If it can’t pay its teachers reliably, it loses them, and the children lose the one stable part of their schooling.

What a gift does

₹10,000, about $115, pays one teacher for one month. $1,400 pays one teacher for a year. Our goal is $14,000: a full year of salaries for all ten.

What we raise goes to teacher salaries at the ashrama.

About us

Upahar Giving is a small volunteer group in New Jersey. Upahar means “gift” in Hindi. We are forming a New Jersey nonprofit and have not yet received 501(c)(3) status, so donations are not tax-deductible for now. We’ll update this page when that changes.
