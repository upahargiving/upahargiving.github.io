Keeping teachers in front of the children at a small school in Karnataka where most have no home to return to.

Shree Shiva Shaikshanika Seva Ashrama is a residential school near Tumakuru. It houses, feeds and teaches children from 1st to 10th standard. About 90% of them have no parents or no safe home. Upahar Giving runs a teaching program there: we engage the teachers and pay each of them directly.

The school

Mr. Lepakshaiah worked as a forest guard near Tumakuru. He had no money and no standing to speak of, but he believed everyone owes something to the people around them. He wanted a place where children without a family could get food, a bed and an education.

Someone gave him a small plot of land and a modest building, free. In 1995 he opened a residential school in it with almost nothing else. It is still running.

When teachers can’t be paid reliably, they leave, and the children lose the one stable part of their schooling.

What a gift does

₹10,000, about $115, pays one teacher for a month. The whole program costs about ₹13.6 lakh a year. Our goal is $16,000: one year of teaching, including transfer fees.

How it works

Each teacher signs a written agreement with Upahar Giving, and we pay them every month, directly into their own bank account. We don’t send money to the school. Indian law restricts foreign funds to charities, and the ashrama isn’t registered to receive them.

We take no commission and have no paid staff. Read the partnership brief for the details.

About us

Upahar Giving Inc. is a volunteer-run nonprofit in New Jersey. Upahar means “gift” in Hindi.

Our 501(c)(3) application is pending. Once it is approved, gifts made since September 13, 2026 will be tax-deductible. Donors in India can also give; write to us for details.
